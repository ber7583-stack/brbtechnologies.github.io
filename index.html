SMS Notification Service

BRB Technologies LLC operates an internal SMS notification system used exclusively for business monitoring and alerts. Messages are sent only to authorized personnel who have explicitly opted in to receive notifications.

SMS Opt-In Process

Recipients of SMS messages from BRB Technologies LLC provide explicit written consent before receiving any messages. The opt-in process works as follows:

  1. The recipient is an authorized employee or owner of BRB Technologies LLC.
  2. The recipient explicitly provides their phone number and requests to receive SMS notifications.
  3. Upon opt-in, a confirmation message is sent to the recipient's phone number.
  4. Recipients may opt out at any time by replying STOP to any message.
  5. For help, recipients may reply HELP at any time.

Message and data rates may apply. Message frequency varies based on business activity.

Privacy Policy

BRB Technologies LLC does not sell, rent, or share phone numbers or personal information with any third parties. Phone numbers collected for SMS notifications are used solely for sending authorized business notifications. All data is stored securely and only accessible to authorized personnel.

Terms & Conditions

By opting in to receive SMS messages from BRB Technologies LLC, you agree to receive automated text messages related to business operations and alerts. You may opt out at any time by replying STOP. For assistance, reply HELP or contact us at brbtechnologiesllc@gmail.com.
